
# Key insights
# Customer Analysis
- The dataset contains **99,457 unique customers**.
- The **top 5 customers** each generated **26,250** in total purchases.
- Together, these top customers contributed **131,250** in revenue, highlighting the presence of high-value customers.

# Category  Performance
- **Clothing** generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- The category contributed **11,399,679.04** in total sales, making it the strongest revenue driver in the dataset.

## Payment Behavior

- Cash was the most frequently used payment method, accounting for 44,447 transactions.
- Cash also generated the highest revenue, contributing 112,832,243.02 in total sales.
- Credit Card was the second most preferred payment method, generating 88,077,123.77 in revenue.
- Debit Card was the least preferred payment method, accounting for 20,079 transactions and generating 50,596,427.46 in revenue.
- The results indicate a strong customer preference for cash transactions, both in transaction volume and revenue contribution.

## Revenue Trends

- Sales revenue varied throughout the year, indicating changing customer purchasing patterns across different months.
- January recorded the highest revenue, generating 28,891,525.59 in total sales.
- November recorded the lowest revenue, with total sales of 18,207,139.95.
- Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year, with monthly sales ranging between 18 million and 29 million.
- Understanding these seasonal trends can help businesses optimize inventory management, staffing, and promotional campaigns.

## Demographic Insights

- Female customers generated the highest revenue, contributing 150,207,136.02 in total sales.
- Male customers contributed 101,298,658.23 in revenue.
- Female customers accounted for a significantly larger share of overall revenue, indicating stronger purchasing activity within this customer segment.
- These insights can help businesses develop more targeted marketing and customer engagement strategies.

## Shopping Mall Performance

- Mall of Istanbul generated the highest revenue among all shopping malls, contributing 50,872,481.68 in total sales.
- The mall outperformed all other locations, making it the strongest revenue-generating shopping destination in the dataset.
- Its strong performance suggests a high concentration of customer activity and purchasing power.

## Age-Based Insights

- The average customer age remained relatively consistent across all product categories, ranging from approximately 43.3 to 43.6 years.
- Cosmetics attracted the oldest customer segment, with an average age of 43.58 years.
- Toys attracted the youngest customer segment, with an average age of 43.33 years.
- The small variation in average age suggests that purchasing behavior was fairly similar across age groups in the dataset.
- These findings indicate that customer age was not a major differentiating factor in product category preferences.
